The experience behind EUROQUEST

Diana Ji, founder of EUROQUEST, has 15 years of experience in immigration consultancy and helping clients move and settle abroad

Her work has involved understanding each client’s needs, finding suitable service providers and following through on practical arrangements across countries and cultures

That experience shapes her approach at EUROQUEST: listening carefully, bringing the right people into the conversation and keeping agreed work on track

Today, she applies those skills to business collaboration between China and Europe, helping companies find suitable partners and work through the practical questions that arise along the way
