/Pickleball & sports venues

Certified pickleball equipment
Venue solutions for Europe

For clubs, venue operators, federations and distributors in Europe, we source pickleball paddles, balls, nets and court flooring direct from vetted Chinese factories, and check EU compliance before anything is quoted

What we source and coordinate

01

Paddles & balls

Only models approved by USA Pickleball or UPA-A, checked model by model before any quote

02

Nets & court equipment

Permanent and portable nets, posts and court accessories for clubs, schools and events

03

Court flooring

Indoor sports flooring with EN 14904 test reports, CE marking and a Declaration of Performance; outdoor surfaces specified for each site

04

Venue solutions

Pickleball lines on padel courts and multi-sport halls, conversions of existing spaces and temporary courts for events

How it works

What we can deliver

  • A compliance checklist by product and model
  • Shortlisted certified options with documents for your review
  • A pilot plan with venue or event layout notes

What we need from you

  • Venue type, number of courts and surface
  • Country and the standards you need to meet
  • Products and quantities
  • Timing, including any tournament or opening date

Scope & responsibilities

Approvals and test reports are checked model by model against official lists and documents; EUROQUEST does not certify products

Manufacturing, installation, line marking and venue works are carried out by the factories and specialist contractors appointed for the project

Shared use of padel or other courts is agreed between the venue owner and the operator
